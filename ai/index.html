Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. We handle the build and hand you a simple interface. If you can use email and a spreadsheet, you can use the agent.

What tools can the agent connect to?

Most modern tools with APIs: Gmail, Google Sheets, Notion, Airtable, HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, Calendly, Stripe, and many more.

What if I do not know which workflow to automate?

That is exactly what the discovery call is for. We will identify the highest-ROI 30-minute task and build around it.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We use private infrastructure, encrypted connections, and your data is never used to train third-party models without permission.
