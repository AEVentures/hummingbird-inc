How Hummingbird works

We build directly in AI. Everywhere else, we back the best.

Hummingbird Inc doesn't need to build every foundation from scratch on day one. In Artificial Intelligence, we operate directly — Hummingbird AI Studio is our own product with our own customers. In other foundations, our role is to identify exceptional independent companies already doing outstanding work, and support them with visibility, awareness, and strategic partnership.

Today that means supporting Nova Trinity in Solar and Westwin Elements in Rare Earth & Critical Minerals. Robotics, Natural Healing, and CRISPR remain part of our long-term vision as we look for the right partners and opportunities in those spaces.
