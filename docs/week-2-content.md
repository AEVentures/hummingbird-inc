## Day 11 — Opinion Post: Agents vs Chatbots

### X Post

AI agents are not chatbots. Here is the real difference:

Chatbot: waits for a prompt, gives an answer.
Agent: watches for events, makes decisions, takes action, and updates systems.

Most "AI tools" sold today are chatbots with nicer branding.

The real leverage comes from agents that run while you sleep.

### LinkedIn Post

There is a lot of confusion in the market right now between AI chatbots and AI agents. They are not the same thing.

A chatbot waits for a prompt and gives an answer. It is useful, but it is reactive.

An agent watches for events, makes decisions, takes action, and updates systems. It is proactive. It operates while you are in meetings, sleeping, or focused on higher-leverage work.

If you are evaluating AI tools for your business, ask one question: does this tool take action, or does it just answer questions? The answer will tell you whether you are buying a novelty or infrastructure.
