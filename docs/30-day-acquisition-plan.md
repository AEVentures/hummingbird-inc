## Daily Operating Cadence

Every day for 30 days:

- **Post 1x on X** (short insight, demo, or opinion).
- **Post or comment 1x on LinkedIn** (professional angle).
- **Engage for 30 minutes** on both platforms: reply to 10–20 relevant accounts.
- **Add 5 prospects** to the outreach tracker.
- **Send 3–5 personalized cold DMs or emails** to warm prospects.
- **Capture metrics** in the tracker at end of day.

Every week:

- **1 long-form pillar** (newsletter/blog/YouTube video).
- **1 customer-facing asset** (case study, testimonial request, or offer refinement).
- **1 outreach campaign** to a specific niche (e.g., real estate agents, family offices, SaaS founders).

## Lead Capture Flow

1. **Organic post** → reader clicks profile.
2. **Profile link** → `hummingbirdinc.io/ai`.
3. **Landing page** → reads offer, clicks "Book Free AI Audit."
4. **Calendly** → books 20-minute call.
5. **Call** → diagnose workflow, present Build Week if fit.
6. **Proposal/Stripe link** → close.

**Alternative path:**

1. **Lead magnet CTA** → "Download the AI Agent Blueprint."
2. **Email capture** → subscribe to newsletter.
3. **Nurture sequence** → 5 emails over 14 days.
4. **Soft CTA** → book audit.
